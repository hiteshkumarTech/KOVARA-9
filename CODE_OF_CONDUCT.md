# Code of Conduct

KOVARA-9 is a research and open-source engineering community. Participation should make technical
disagreement safer, more precise, and more useful.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient with people of different experience levels.
- Critique ideas, code, methods, and evidence—not people.
- State uncertainty and distinguish verified results from hypotheses or illustrative examples.
- Credit prior work and preserve contributor attribution.
- Protect private information and remove credentials or sensitive data from logs.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, scientific integrity, and repository safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, sustained
personal attacks, spam, and knowingly fabricated research claims are not acceptable in project
spaces or when representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary project disputes, contact the repository maintainers through GitHub. For conduct or
security matters that should not be public, use GitHub's private reporting features where available
and do not open a public issue containing sensitive details.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates
this code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be considered as
privately and consistently as the available platform permits. Retaliation against a reporter is a
violation of this code.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces and in public spaces when an individual is officially
representing KOVARA-9. It does not replace GitHub's terms or policies.
